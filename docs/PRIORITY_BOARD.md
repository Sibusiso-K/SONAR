## Tier 1 — clear the calendar

### 1. BCG Platinion Hackathon — score 7.9
**Why it's number one.** Nothing else on this board puts you in a room in Johannesburg with BCG consultants for two days with your travel paid. BCG Platinion has publicly documented a participant converting from hackathon attendee into a Visiting Associate role. Career leverage 10/10, and winnability is unusually high at 8/10 because selection is by application (CV + motivation letter), not by beating 10,000 people on a leaderboard.

**The catch you must not miss:** you apply **individually**. You and Lethabo submit separate applications. Teams are formed on-site to force networking. Do not try to apply as a pair.

**Deadline conflict:** the landing-page banner says "Apply by September 7"; the body text says September 13. **Treat 7 September as real.** Apply the week of 24 August, well ahead of both.

**Prep:** a CV tuned toward AI/tech consulting, and a motivation letter that ties food security to something you've actually built. If you place well at ADTC first, that goes in the letter.

### 2. Africa Deep Tech Challenge — score 7.65
**Why:** $16,500 cash, and the scoring formula is a gift to a small disciplined team.

```
S = 0.50·Accuracy + 0.30·Throughput + 0.20·Efficiency − 10 (thermal penalty)
```

Half the score is **not** model quality. Throughput is measured against a 15 tokens/sec reference, and efficiency rewards you for staying under 7GB RAM. A team that picks a 1–3B parameter model, quantises it aggressively, and does careful cache and memory work will beat a team that brings a bigger, smarter model and thrashes swap. Most of the 1,540 registrants will chase accuracy and get destroyed on the other 50%.

**Domain pick.** Choose one: Math & Scientific Reasoning, Healthcare, Agriculture, Creative Writing, Coding Assistants, Corporate/Enterprise, or Autonomous AI Agents. **Agriculture or Healthcare** — they're the strongest fit for the separate $1,500 "Best African Use Case" prize and there are validation sets for each.

**Day one actions:** clone the [submission template](https://github.com/Africa-Deep-Tech-Foundation/adtc-2026-submission-template), clone the [profiler](https://github.com/Africa-Deep-Tech-Foundation/adtc-profiler), and [apply for the free GPU credits](https://aghcloud.ai/p/3850810a-8f62-474c-b448-e204d026b189). The profiler is what tells you whether you're being disqualified for OOM before the judges do.

**Hard rule:** OOM or sandbox crash = disqualification. Test on 8GB before you submit, not after.

### 3. IBM Dev Day: Bob in Action — score 7.35
**Why:** the best odds-to-reward ratio on the entire board. **The top 50 qualified submissions each get a TechXchange 2026 conference pass per team member.** Not the top 3 — the top 50. And the audience is IBM i and IBM Z enterprise developers, a small and conservative crowd. A modern developer-workflow angle stands out sharply here in a way it never would on Devpost.

**Timing:** virtual enablement session 27 August, hackathon 28–30 August. That's the weekend right after ADTC closes — which works, because ADTC submits on the 24th/25th.

**Do before 27 August:** [request the free Bob trial](https://bob.ibm.com/trial) and [register](https://ibmdevday-bob-platform.bemyapp.com/#/register). Attend the 10:00 ET enablement session (16:00 SAST) — it's explicitly a hackathon prep session.

**What wins:** they ask for Agent mode, parallel tasks, subagents, and document understanding — not code completion. Pick one painful workflow (onboarding a new dev onto a legacy codebase is the obvious one for an IBM i/Z audience) and show a measured before/after.
