## REEFPRINT: technical proof and ten-minute presentation

SONAR describes an abstract for optical proxies trained from laboratory mineral maps, five Bushveld phases and plant recommendations with abstention. These are commitments, not benchmark results. The War Room's “sub-1%” refers to class abundance; it is not a recall score.

Preparation evidence to collect from the actual project:

- Data provenance and permission; paired optical/teacher-map alignment and a split that prevents related specimen imagery leaking across train/test sets.
- Per-class recall and sample support for chromite, orthopyroxene, plagioclase, base-metal sulphide, and talc/serpentine. State the rare-class sample count and uncertainty; never substitute aggregate accuracy for this claim.
- Measured runtime on the demonstration hardware, plus failure cases and domain-shift limitations.
- Calibration/abstention behavior: show a case where the system declines a recommendation; state how thresholds were chosen.
- A clear distinction between measured mineral-map performance and hypothesized downstream process benefits. Label liberation, reagent-demand and grindability relationships by their actual validation status.
- Impact calculation with inputs, units and source dates. Verify current tariffs and emissions/tax assumptions before numerical claims; this pack does not validate those rates or claim savings.
- Integration boundary for StarCS/FloatStar/MillStar. Describe a proposed interface unless an actual integration has been demonstrated.
